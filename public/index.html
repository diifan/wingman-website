What is WingMan?+

WingMan is an AI conversation companion for iPhone. It helps you find reply ideas, develop a personal writing profile and organize conversation context. It also includes a custom keyboard for supported apps.

Will the replies actually sound like me?+

WingMan uses the writing examples and context you choose to provide to personalize suggestions. Results can vary, so review and edit every reply until it feels right for you.

Does WingMan send messages for me?+

You choose the reply and decide what to send. WingMan offers suggestions; it does not independently manage your conversations.

What happens to my screenshots and messages?+

Screenshot text is recognized on your device; the original images are not uploaded for AI processing. With your consent, selected text and writing-profile information are sent to WingMan and Alibaba Cloud (Qwen) to generate suggestions. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I control what WingMan learns?+

Yes. You can manage writing-profile information and typing-learning settings in the app, delete saved contact information, and withdraw AI consent under AI & privacy.

How can I get WingMan?+

WingMan is in development for iPhone. For availability and product questions, email hello@goswie.com.
