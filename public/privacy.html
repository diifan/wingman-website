WINGMAN / PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Scope and contact

This policy describes information handling for the WingMan app and website, provided by SWINK AI INC. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@goswie.com. The information involved depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

2. Information you provide

WingMan can work with messages, text extracted from screenshots, practice replies, writing profiles, saved conversation context and contact preferences. These may include personal information about you or other people. We also receive information you send when contacting support. Only provide information you are entitled to use and avoid unnecessary sensitive details.

3. Accounts and sign-in

WingMan uses Supabase for account authentication and profile storage. Email sign-in involves your email address and a protected password verifier. Google or Apple sign-in provides account identifiers and, when available, your email and name. Login tokens are stored in your device’s Keychain. Signing in does not by itself upload your saved screenshots, conversations or writing profile.

4. AI processing and screenshots

With your permission, text you select, practice replies and writing-profile information are sent to WingMan and Alibaba Cloud (Qwen) to personalize suggestions. Screenshot text recognition runs on your device; original screenshot images are not uploaded for this processing. WingMan does not store chat text on its AI gateway server. The AI provider processes requests under its own service terms. This is not a promise that all processing stays on your device. You can withdraw AI consent under AI & privacy.

5. Keyboard and writing profiles

When enabled, typing with the WingMan keyboard saves your own drafts under the selected contact on your device. Completed typing can be used to learn your wording even when message delivery cannot be verified; active drafts and AI suggestions are excluded from learning. You can manage typing-learning settings, delete writing-profile information and remove saved contact data. Clipboard access and keyboard functionality are subject to iOS permissions. Saved conversations and writing profiles are device-local and are not automatically switched or synchronized between accounts.

6. Why information is used and shared

Information is processed to provide the features you request, personalize suggestions, authenticate accounts, provide support and protect service security. Providers may process information needed for hosting, authentication and AI requests. Website and online requests may expose technical information such as IP addresses, device or browser details, timestamps and errors to the systems handling them. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to address threats or misuse. Messages you send through another app are subject to that app’s practices.

7. Retention, deletion and security

You can remove local conversation and writing-profile data using the app’s controls. Account deletion is available in the account screen; local conversation data is separate from account storage. Contact us to request deletion of other information we hold. Retention depends on purpose and applicable legal or security requirements. Deleting a local copy does not remove messages already sent to other people or held by other services. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

Manage keyboard, photo and other permissions in device settings. You can withdraw AI consent and control optional writing learning in the app. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain your information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent or complain to a data protection authority. Send requests to hello@goswie.com; we may need to verify your authority.

9. Children, international processing and updates

Do not provide children’s personal information without the permissions required in your circumstances. Contact us if you believe a child’s information has been submitted improperly. Service providers may process information outside your country, subject to applicable transfer requirements. We will update this page’s date and provide appropriate notice of material changes. Read our Terms of Service for conditions of use.
